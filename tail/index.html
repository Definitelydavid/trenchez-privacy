Privacy Policy

TAIL browser extension · Effective 18 September 2026

TAIL shows which FOMO traders hold the token you are viewing on supported trading sites, and lets you follow (“tail”) traders to be notified of their trades. It does not trade, hold keys or move funds, and it has no account or server of its own.

What stays on your device

Your tail list, settings, the recent trade tape and — if you add one — your fomoapi.io API key are stored in your browser's extension storage (chrome.storage). They are never sent to us.

What leaves your device, and why

ServiceWhat is sentWhy
fomoapi.io (api.fomoapi.io)The address of the token you are viewing; your fomoapi key if you added oneList the FOMO traders holding it, the leaderboard, and the live trade stream
DexScreener (api.dexscreener.com)The token or pair address you are viewingMarket cap and price, to compute entry market cap

These services receive your IP address as part of any web request and are governed by their own privacy policies.

Pages the extension reads

On GMGN, Axiom, Dexscreener and MevX, TAIL reads the page address to know which token you are viewing. It does not read page content, form fields, cookies, logins or browsing history, and it does not record what you view. It never accesses fomo.family.

What we don't do

No analytics, no advertising, no sale or sharing of data, no tracking across sites. TAIL is not affiliated with FOMO.

Your control

Remove traders or your key in the extension's Settings. Uninstalling the extension removes all stored data.

Contact

Through the support link on the Chrome Web Store listing.
